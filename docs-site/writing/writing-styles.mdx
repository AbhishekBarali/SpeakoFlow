A writing style controls how cleaned dictation *sounds*. It's applied on top of [AI cleanup](/writing/ai-cleanup) and stays separate from your cleanup prompt, so you can mix any prompt with any style. Pick one in *Settings → Dictation → AI cleanup → Writing style*.

## Built-in styles

*Default: None (cleanup only).*

- **None (cleanup only)** (default) — fixes the text without intentionally changing its voice.
- **Formal** — polished, grammatically complete sentences; slang and casual shorthand replaced with precise neutral wording. It won't become ceremonial or legalistic unless what you said already was.
- **Casual** — relaxed and conversational, with everyday wording and ordinary contractions. It won't invent slang, jokes, or familiarity that wasn't there.
- **Professional** — direct, courteous, and easy to act on, with rambling removed. Never longer than the original, and never sales-like.
- **Friendly** — warm and approachable, without adding compliments, emoji, exclamation marks, or enthusiasm you didn't express.
- **Concise** — as brief as possible, cutting repetition, hedging, and wordiness while keeping every material fact, condition, name, number, and deadline.

Every built-in style preserves your meaning, facts, and point of view, and keeps the length close to what you said. The exception is Concise, which is explicitly allowed to shorten — but still not to drop a detail.

## Creating your own

The built-ins are fixed, but you can save any number of your own styles beside them. In the **Writing style** section, choose *Create style* and give it:

### Style name

For example, "Calm and clean."

### Style instructions

Describe precisely how the wording should change. For example: *"Remove profanity and replace it with calm, neutral wording."*

Both fields are required. Your new style is selected as soon as you save it, and it appears in the same dropdown as the built-ins from then on. Select it and use *Edit* to change or delete it; built-in styles have no edit button, because there is nothing stored to edit.

<Note>
  Style instructions shape the wording *after* cleanup, and they never replace your cleanup prompt — both are sent to the model together. Cleanup strength is a third, independent dial: it decides how much of your rambling gets tidied away, while the style decides the register of whatever survives.
</Note>
