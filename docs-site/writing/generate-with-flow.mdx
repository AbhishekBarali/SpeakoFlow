Start a dictation with "Hey Flow" and describe what you want. SpeakoFlow writes the finished thing and pastes it, instead of typing your words.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    _Settings → Dictation → Generate with Flow_. It is off by default.
  </Step>

<Step title="Hold your Dictate shortcut and lead with the phrase">
  "Hey Flow, write a polite email asking to reschedule Friday's call to Monday."
</Step>

  <Step title="Let go">
    The finished email is pasted where your cursor is.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The phrase only counts at the very start of a dictation. Say it anywhere else and the words paste as ordinary dictation, so Flow never fires by accident.

Flow runs on your normal **Dictate** shortcut only. The [AI cleanup](/writing/ai-cleanup) shortcut keeps doing what it always does, and cleanup never runs over Flow output.

## Settings

Three settings appear in _Settings → Dictation_ once you turn Flow on:

| Setting                 | Default  | What it does                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Generate with Flow**  | Off      | The feature itself.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Activation phrase**   | Hey Flow | The words that trigger it. Change it to anything you like. Clearing the field resets it to "Hey Flow".                                                                   |
| **Allow screen access** | Off      | Lets Flow take one screenshot when a command clearly refers to your screen, like "look at this email and draft a reply". The model decides whether it needs the capture. |

<Note>
  Flow uses your assistant's model and provider. Without one, your words paste
  as ordinary dictation and the overlay says why. Set one up on the
  [Models](/models/transcription-models) page or under
  [Providers](/assistant/providers).
</Note>

Generate with Flow survives the assistant master switch. Turning the assistant off does not disable Flow, because they share a provider, not a feature gate.

## Screen access

With **Allow screen access** on, Flow can take one screenshot when a command clearly refers to your screen, like "look at this and draft a reply". The model decides whether it needs the capture; for a command it can answer without looking, it writes directly.

Flow captures the monitor your mouse pointer is on, so a multi-monitor setup gets the screen you are working on.

This is permissioned separately from the assistant's screen access. Turning on screen vision for the assistant does not give Flow a camera, and vice versa. See [Screen vision](/assistant/screen-vision) for the assistant's controls.

## Phrase matching

Matching ignores case and punctuation, and it tolerates the spellings speech-to-text actually produces. "Hey Flo", "hey FLO", and "Hey, Flow:" all trigger it, because a wake word should match by sound rather than by whichever spelling the model picked.

The tolerance is bounded. A candidate word has to share the first letter, a similar sound, and be close in length. So "Hey follow the instructions" and "Hey floor plans are ready" do not trigger Flow.

Once the phrase is heard in the live overlay, it is rewritten to match your configured spelling as you speak, so "Hey Flo," shows up as "Hey Flow,".

## If nothing gets pasted

Flow is all or nothing. It pastes a complete result or it pastes nothing at all. You never get partial output, error text, or your raw spoken command.

| What happened                                    | What you see                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| No assistant model configured                    | Ordinary dictation, plus an overlay notice               |
| You said the phrase with no command after it     | Nothing pasted                                           |
| Generation failed, timed out, or came back empty | Nothing pasted, the overlay says Flow could not generate |
| You cancelled                                    | Nothing pasted                                           |

Every Flow recording is saved under the **Flow** filter in [History](/personalize/history), including failed and cancelled ones, so you never lose the thought.
