Speak another language and get clean English out — on your device, with a capable model.

<ResponseField name="Translate to English" type="toggle" default="off">
  Translate speech from other languages to English as it's transcribed. Availability depends on the model — translation-capable models include Whisper, Canary, and Cohere. If your current model doesn't support it, the setting notes that and you can switch models.
</ResponseField>

See [Languages & accents](/models/languages) for which model to pick for which languages, and [Transcription models](/models/transcription-models) for the full catalog.
