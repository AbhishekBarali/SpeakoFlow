Lock recording so you can let go of the keys and keep talking — useful for long dictation or when your hands leave the keyboard.

While you're *holding* your record shortcut, tap the **tap-to-lock key** (`Space` by default) once. Recording locks hands-free, so you can release the shortcut and keep talking. Press the shortcut again to stop and type it out.

## Tap to lock

*Default: Space.*

Pick any key that isn't part of your record shortcut, or clear it to turn the feature off. The assistant shortcut has its own separate tap-to-lock key.

The overlay shows *"Recording hands-free — press the hotkey again to stop"* once you're locked, so you always know which mode you're in.

<Note>
  Tap-to-lock is separate from the **Tap** recording behavior in [General settings](/settings/general). Tap-to-lock works while you're in Hold mode, so you don't have to switch modes to go hands-free once.
</Note>
