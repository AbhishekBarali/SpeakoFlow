SpeakoFlow has one recording-behavior setting that controls every shortcut: dictation, dictation with cleanup, ask the assistant, and start a conversation. Find it on the **Home** page (in the shortcuts card) or in _Settings → Shortcuts_, labelled "Recording behavior".

## Hold (default)

Records while you hold a recording shortcut, stops when you release. Best for short bursts, and it cannot leave a recording running by accident.

## Tap

One press starts recording, the next press stops it and inserts the text. Releases are ignored, so nothing depends on how long you hold the keys. Best for long-form dictation or dictating hands-free.

## Stopping and cancelling

| Action                              | What happens                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Release the keys (Hold mode)        | Stops recording and inserts the text.                                                        |
| Press the shortcut again (Tap mode) | Stops recording and inserts the text.                                                        |
| Press <kbd>Esc</kbd>                | Cancels the recording. The pill offers Undo for a few seconds (see [Undo](/dictation/undo)). |
| Do nothing                          | The recording stops itself after 30 minutes.                                                 |

<kbd>Esc</kbd> is the default Cancel shortcut on Windows and macOS. It only acts
while something is running, like a recording or a reply being read aloud, so
other apps keep their Esc the rest of the time. Cancel is not available on Linux
yet.

To change or turn off Cancel, click its keys on the **Home** page or in _Settings → Shortcuts_. The editor that opens has "Use default" and "Turn off". The conversation shortcut can also be turned off from there.

<Note>
  On a Mac, set **System Settings → Keyboard → Press 🌐 key to** to **Do
  Nothing**, or the globe key opens the emoji picker as well. Macs that were on
  the older Option-based defaults keep them after updating.
</Note>
