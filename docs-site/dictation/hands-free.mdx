title: "Hands-free recording"
description: "Switch SpeakoFlow's recording behavior from Hold to Tap so one press starts recording and the next stops it, letting your hands leave the keyboard during long dictation."
icon: "hand"
---

Record without holding the keys down — useful for long dictation, or when your hands leave the keyboard.

Set *Settings → General → Recording → Recording behavior* to **Tap**. One press of your record shortcut starts recording; the next press stops it and types the transcript out. Releases are ignored, so nothing depends on how long you hold the keys.

## Hold vs. Tap

- **Hold** (default) — records while the shortcut is held, stops on release. Best for short bursts, and it can't leave a recording running by accident.
- **Tap** — press once to start, press again to stop. Best for long-form dictation.

The setting is global: it applies to the Dictate shortcut, the AI-cleanup shortcut, and the Assistant shortcut alike.

When a hands-free recording starts, the overlay pill switches to a calmer, slightly larger waveform, and screen readers announce *"Recording hands-free — press the hotkey again to stop."* There is no stop button on the overlay: press the shortcut again.

<Note>
  In the assistant panel, a voice turn can also be ended with the **Finish & send** button, so a hands-free question can be committed without touching the keyboard.
</Note>

## Stopping and cancelling

- **Press the shortcut again** — stops recording and inserts the text.
- **Cancel shortcut** — throws the recording away. It has **no default binding**, because a global `Esc` would swallow `Esc` presses meant for other apps whenever a recording is active. Record a key for it in *Settings → General → Recording* if you want one. The row is hidden on Linux, where re-registering a dynamic shortcut is unreliable.
- **Nothing at all** — a recording is force-finalized after 30 minutes.

<Warning>
  A mid-recording "tap to lock" gesture — tapping `Shift` or `Space` while holding the shortcut to convert a held recording to hands-free — is not active in the current build. The settings keys for it still exist in the settings file, but nothing arms the watcher, so the gesture does nothing and there is no control for it in Settings. Use the **Tap** recording behavior above instead.
</Warning>
