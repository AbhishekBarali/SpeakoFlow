A small on-screen indicator that shows SpeakoFlow is listening, so you're never guessing whether it heard you.

## Overlay style

Set in *Settings → General → Overlay*. Controls how the recording overlay looks while you dictate:

- **None** — hides the overlay entirely.
- **Minimal** — a compact pill that shows recording state.
- **Live** — a readable card that shows your words as you speak (for models that support it).

## Overlay position

Where the overlay sits on your screen: **Bottom** (default), **Top**, or **None**.

## Status while you work

The overlay also surfaces status as things happen — *Getting mic ready*, *Recording*, *Transcribing*, *Writing with Flow*, or *Looking at your screen* — and lets you cancel in progress.

<Warning>
  **Linux.** On native GNOME/Wayland the overlay can't always stay on top of other windows. SpeakoFlow works around this automatically — see [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting).
</Warning>
