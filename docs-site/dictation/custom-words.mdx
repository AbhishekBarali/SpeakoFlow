Teach SpeakoFlow to spell names, products, and phrases your way.

Add names, terms, or short phrases that the model tends to get wrong, and SpeakoFlow will correct them in your transcripts. For example, add `Kokoro` if it keeps writing "Coco row."

<Tip>
  Custom words shine for non-Western names and specialized vocabulary — great for names from Nepali, Indian, and other languages that generic models spell phonetically. Pair them with a strong multilingual model like Whisper for the best results.
</Tip>

The sensitivity of custom-word correction can be tuned with the [word correction threshold](/settings/debug) in Debug settings.
