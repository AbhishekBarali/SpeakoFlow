title: "Live vs. batch transcription"
description: "SpeakoFlow streams text as you speak on streaming-capable models and transcribes in a single batch on the rest. Which models stream, and how to change the behavior."
icon: "tower-broadcast"
---

Your words can land as you speak them, or all at once when you stop. Which one you get depends on the model you're running.

On a streaming-capable model, live transcription is on out of the box — text appears while you're still talking. Every other model transcribes in one batch when you finish, which stays accurate and simple.

## Live transcription

*Default: on for streaming models, off for the rest.*

Transcribe speech as you talk instead of all at once when you stop. This only takes effect on a model that supports streaming — on any other model SpeakoFlow batches regardless of the setting, so there's no harm in leaving it on.

## Live transcription window

*Default: off.*

While live transcription runs, show a larger overlay card with the running text instead of the compact pill. Requires live transcription.

<Note>
  The streaming-capable models are the Moonshine V2 variants and the Nemotron streaming model. See [Transcription models](/models/transcription-models) for the full catalog and what each one is good at.
</Note>

Switching to a non-streaming model, like Whisper Large for its accuracy, moves you back to batch transcription automatically. Nothing to reconfigure.
