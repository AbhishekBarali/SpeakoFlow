Choose whether words appear as you speak, or all at once when you stop.

By default, SpeakoFlow transcribes in a single batch when you finish — accurate and simple. With live transcription on, text streams in as you talk (on models that support streaming).

<ResponseField name="Live transcription" type="toggle" default="off">
  **Experimental.** Transcribe speech as you talk, instead of all at once when you stop.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Live transcription window" type="toggle" default="off">
  While live transcription runs, show a larger overlay card with the running text instead of the compact pill. Requires live transcription.
</ResponseField>

<Note>
  Streaming-capable models (like the Moonshine V2 and Nemotron streaming models) are the best fit for live mode. See [Transcription models](/models/transcription-models) for which ones stream.
</Note>

Live transcription is an experimental feature, so it's hidden until you turn on **Experimental features** in [Debug & advanced](/settings/debug).
