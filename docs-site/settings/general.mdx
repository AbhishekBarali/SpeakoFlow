## Appearance

### Theme

*Light, Dark, System.*

A theme for the app and the assistant panel.

### Text size

*Small, Default, Large, Extra large.*

Scale text and controls across the app.

### App language

Change the language of the SpeakoFlow interface. This is separate from your speech-recognition language.

## Recording & input

### Shortcuts

The keyboard shortcuts that start and stop recording and open the assistant. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/start/keyboard-shortcuts) for the full list and defaults.

### Recording behavior

*Hold, Tap. Default: Hold.*

**Hold** records while the shortcut is pressed; **Tap** starts with one press and stops with the next.

### Tap to lock

*Default: Space.*

The key you tap mid-hold to go hands-free. See [Hands-free & tap-to-lock](/dictation/hands-free).

### Language

*Auto, specific language. Default: Auto.*

Speech-recognition language. A specific language can improve accuracy. See [Languages & accents](/models/languages).

### Microphone

Select your preferred microphone device.

## Sounds

### Audio feedback

*Default: off.*

Play a sound when recording starts and stops.

### Feedback sound

*Default, Marimba, Pop, Click, more.*

Choose the start/stop cue — several themes are bundled, with a preview button.

### Output device

Which device plays feedback sounds.

### Volume

Adjust the volume of audio feedback sounds.

## Startup & tray

### Launch on startup

Open SpeakoFlow automatically when you sign in.

### Start hidden

Launch to the tray without opening the window.

### Show tray icon

Keep SpeakoFlow in your system tray. The tray menu offers Home, Check for updates, Copy last transcript, Unload model, and Quit.

## Updates

### Check for updates

Let SpeakoFlow check for new versions and offer to download them. Turn off to disable automatic checks.
