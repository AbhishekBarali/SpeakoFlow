Shortcuts, your microphone, sounds, startup, and how the app looks. Everything in the *General* section, grouped as you'll find it.

  <img src="https://www.speakoflow.com/general-dark.png" alt="The SpeakoFlow Settings window open to the General section, shown in dark mode, with Recording, Appearance, Sounds, and Overlay groups." />

## Appearance

<ResponseField name="Theme" type="Light | Dark | System">
  A theme for the app and the assistant panel.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Text size" type="Small | Default | Large | Extra large">
  Scale text and controls across the app.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="App language" type="language">
  Change the language of the SpeakoFlow interface. This is separate from your speech-recognition language.
</ResponseField>

## Recording & input

<ResponseField name="Shortcuts" type="key combinations">
  The keyboard shortcuts that start and stop recording and open the assistant. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/start/keyboard-shortcuts) for the full list and defaults.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Recording behavior" type="Hold | Tap" default="Hold">
  **Hold** records while the shortcut is pressed; **Tap** starts with one press and stops with the next.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Tap to lock" type="key" default="Space">
  The key you tap mid-hold to go hands-free. See [Hands-free & tap-to-lock](/dictation/hands-free).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Language" type="Auto | specific language" default="Auto">
  Speech-recognition language. A specific language can improve accuracy. See [Languages & accents](/models/languages).
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Microphone" type="device">
  Select your preferred microphone device.
</ResponseField>

## Sounds

<ResponseField name="Audio feedback" type="toggle" default="off">
  Play a sound when recording starts and stops.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Feedback sound" type="Default | Marimba | Pop | Click | more">
  Choose the start/stop cue — several themes are bundled, with a preview button.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Output device" type="device">
  Which device plays feedback sounds.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Volume" type="percentage">
  Adjust the volume of audio feedback sounds.
</ResponseField>

## Startup & tray

<ResponseField name="Launch on startup" type="toggle">
  Open SpeakoFlow automatically when you sign in.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Start hidden" type="toggle">
  Launch to the tray without opening the window.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Show tray icon" type="toggle">
  Keep SpeakoFlow in your system tray. The tray menu offers Home, Check for updates, Copy last transcript, Unload model, and Quit.
</ResponseField>

## Updates

<ResponseField name="Check for updates" type="toggle">
  Let SpeakoFlow check for new versions and offer to download them. Turn off to disable automatic checks.
</ResponseField>
