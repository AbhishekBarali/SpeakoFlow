A quick mental model that makes the rest of the docs click into place.

## One hotkey layer over your real work

SpeakoFlow sits quietly in your system tray and does nothing until you press a shortcut. It's a layer over whatever app you're already in — you never switch to it to dictate. Three jobs live under that hotkey layer:

- **[Dictation](/dictation/basics)** — local speech-to-text typed into the active app. Your audio never leaves the machine.
- **[Generate with Flow](/writing/generate-with-flow)** — start a dictation with an activation phrase and the AI writes the finished result.
- **[The assistant](/assistant/panel)** — a floating panel you summon to ask questions by voice or text, optionally with screen vision and spoken answers.

## The dictation pipeline

When you dictate, your audio flows through a local pipeline:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your microphone" icon="microphone">
    Audio is captured from the input device you selected in [General settings](/settings/general).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Voice-activity detection" icon="filter">
    Silero VAD filters out silence, so pauses while you think don't become gaps in your text.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Speech-to-text, on your hardware" icon="microchip">
    whisper.cpp or Parakeet runs the model on your GPU, or your CPU if no GPU is available. See [Acceleration & GPU](/models/acceleration).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Text into the active app" icon="clipboard">
    Inserted via the clipboard or typed directly. See [Output & pasting](/dictation/output).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing in that chain touches the network. The two things that can add a network hop after it are both opt-in and clearly marked: [AI cleanup](/writing/ai-cleanup) and [Generate with Flow](/writing/generate-with-flow) hand the text to whichever model provider you configured, which may be the fully local built-in engine.

## What the assistant does with a question

An assistant turn isn't a single request. The model is given a small set of tools — web search and the current date and time — and decides for itself whether it needs them, up to a bounded number of rounds so a turn can't loop indefinitely. Web search is only offered as a tool when you've turned it on, so with it off the assistant simply answers from what it knows.

Screen vision works the same way: nothing is captured unless you ask for it, and only the monitor under your mouse cursor is grabbed. The full-resolution frame goes to the model once; only a small thumbnail is kept locally so you can see what it looked at.

## Local-first, your choice

Speech-to-text is always local. The assistant is where you choose your comfort level:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Built-in (offline)" icon="microchip">
    Download a small local model and run it fully on your machine. No key, no network.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Local server" icon="server">
    Point SpeakoFlow at Ollama or LM Studio running on your own machine.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Cloud" icon="cloud">
    Bring your own API key for any OpenAI-compatible provider.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

See [Brain: local or cloud](/assistant/brain) to set this up, and [Privacy](/reference/privacy) for exactly what is stored and where.

## Settings, organized the same way

The Settings window has five sections, and a sixth that only appears once you turn on debug mode:

- **[General](/settings/general)** — shortcuts, microphone, appearance, language, and the Experimental features toggle
- **Dictation** — the model, [AI cleanup](/writing/ai-cleanup), [Generate with Flow](/writing/generate-with-flow), and text handling
- **Assistant** — [brain](/assistant/brain), [voice](/assistant/voice-output), [screen vision](/assistant/screen-vision), [web search](/assistant/web-search), plus [Profiles](/personalize/profiles) and [Memory](/personalize/memory) as drill-down pages
- **[History](/personalize/history)** — past dictations, Flow generations, and assistant chats
- **[About](/reference/about)** — version, update check, license, and where your data lives
- **[Debug](/settings/debug)** — hidden until you press `Ctrl` + `Shift` + `D` (`Cmd` + `Shift` + `D` on macOS) with the settings window focused
