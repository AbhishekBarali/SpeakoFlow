## One hotkey layer over your real work

SpeakoFlow sits quietly in your system tray and does nothing until you press a shortcut. It's a layer over whatever app you're already in — you never switch to it to dictate. Three jobs live under that hotkey layer:

- **[Dictation](/dictation/basics)** — local speech-to-text typed into the active app. Your audio never leaves the machine.
- **[Generate with Flow](/writing/generate-with-flow)** — start a dictation with an activation phrase and the AI writes the finished result.
- **[The assistant](/assistant/panel)** — a floating panel you summon to ask questions by voice or text, optionally with screen vision and spoken answers.

## The dictation pipeline

When you dictate, your audio flows through a local pipeline:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your microphone" icon="microphone">
    Audio is captured from the input device you selected in [General settings](/settings/general).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Voice-activity detection" icon="filter">
    Silence is filtered out, so pauses while you think don't become gaps in your text.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Speech-to-text, on your hardware" icon="microchip">
    The model runs on your GPU, or your CPU if no GPU is available. See [Acceleration & GPU](/models/acceleration).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Text into the active app" icon="clipboard">
    Inserted via the clipboard or typed directly. See [Output & pasting](/dictation/output).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing in that chain touches the network.

## Local-first, your choice

Speech-to-text is always local. The assistant is where you choose your comfort level:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Built-in (offline)" icon="microchip">
    Download a small local model and run it fully on your machine. No key, no network.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Local server" icon="server">
    Point SpeakoFlow at Ollama or LM Studio running on your own machine.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Cloud" icon="cloud">
    Bring your own API key for any OpenAI-compatible provider.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

See [Brain: local or cloud](/assistant/brain) to set this up, and [Privacy](/reference/privacy) for exactly what is stored and where.
