SpeakoFlow sits in your system tray and does nothing until you press a shortcut. You never switch to it to dictate. It is a layer over whatever app you are already in.

## The dictation pipeline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Microphone" icon="microphone">
    Audio is captured from the input device you picked in _Settings → Audio_.
  </Step>

<Step title="Voice activity detection" icon="filter">
  Silero VAD filters out silence, so pauses while you think do not become gaps
  in your text.
</Step>

  <Step title="Speech to text" icon="microchip">
    A local model (Parakeet, Whisper, Nemotron, or one of the other 65 in the catalog) turns the audio into text, using your GPU or your CPU. See [Acceleration](/models/acceleration).

    If you pick a [cloud speech service](/dictation/cloud-transcription) instead, the audio is sent to that provider. Nothing else in the pipeline changes.

  </Step>

<Step title="AI cleanup (optional)" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
  When [AI cleanup](/writing/ai-cleanup) is on, the transcript is handed to a
  language model that removes filler, fixes grammar, and follows spoken edits.
  This runs on whichever model you chose for cleanup: the built-in engine (fully
  local), your own Ollama or LM Studio server, or a cloud provider.
</Step>

  <Step title="Text into the active app" icon="clipboard">
    The finished text is pasted at your cursor via the clipboard, or typed directly. See [Output](/dictation/output).
  </Step>
</Steps>

With a local speech model and the built-in cleanup engine, nothing in that chain touches the network.

## The assistant

When you ask the assistant a question (the [quick ask](/assistant/panel)), the question goes to whichever model you chose:

- **Built-in engine.** A local llama.cpp process. No key, no network.
- **A local server.** Ollama or LM Studio on your own machine.
- **A cloud provider.** Your own API key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. See [Providers](/assistant/providers).

The model gets a small set of tools (the current date and time, plus [web search](/assistant/web-search) and [screen capture](/assistant/screen-vision) if you turned them on) and decides per question whether it needs them. The loop is bounded, so a turn cannot run away.

The quick ask is one question and one answer, with Copy, Insert, or Replace. [Conversations](/assistant/conversation) are the longer-form mode: you talk, the assistant answers out loud, and you can go back and forth hands-free.

## What stays local, what goes to the cloud

| Data                | Local model                                                       | Cloud provider                                                                 |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Voice recordings    | Processed on your machine. Never uploaded.                        | Sent to the speech service you chose.                                          |
| Transcripts         | Stay on your machine.                                             | Stay on your machine after the response.                                       |
| Assistant questions | Processed on your machine.                                        | Sent to the provider, along with any selected text.                            |
| Screenshots         | Captured and discarded locally if the model does not ask for one. | Sent to the provider only when the model asks for one and screen vision is on. |
| Web search queries  | Not applicable.                                                   | Sent to the search provider you chose.                                         |
| Memory              | Stored on your machine. Never uploaded.                           | Used in the prompt sent to the provider, if memory is on.                      |

There is no telemetry, no analytics, and no account. More detail is in [Privacy](/reference/privacy).
