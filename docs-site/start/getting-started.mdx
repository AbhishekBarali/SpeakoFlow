Install the app, pick a model, and dictate your first sentence. The whole thing takes a couple of minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install" icon="download">
    Download SpeakoFlow for your platform and install it. Full per-platform instructions, including the one-time macOS Terminal command, are on [Install SpeakoFlow](/start/install).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run the setup wizard" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    On first launch, a short wizard walks you through two steps:

    1. **Hear you.** Choose a speech-to-text model. This turns your voice into text right on your machine. Pick one now — you can switch anytime in Settings. A fast English model or a real-time multilingual model are good starting points.
    2. **Give it a brain (optional).** Optionally download a small local model for the assistant and AI cleanup. You can skip this and set it up later under *Settings → Assistant → On my device*.

    Models download in the background. You can start using the app right away, and any other model is one click away in Settings. See [Transcription models](/models/transcription-models) for the full catalog.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grant permissions" icon="lock">
    SpeakoFlow needs two permissions to work:

    - **Microphone access** — required to hear your voice for transcription.
    - **Accessibility access** (macOS) — required to type transcribed text into your applications.

    If you skip these during setup, you'll be prompted again the first time they're needed. See [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting) if a permission gets stuck.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Say your first sentence" icon="microphone">
    Open any text field — an email, an editor, a chat box — then hold your Dictate shortcut and talk. Let go, and your words type themselves. That's the whole loop.

    | Action | Windows | macOS | Linux |
    | --- | --- | --- | --- |
    | Dictate | `Left Ctrl` + `Left Win` | `Option` + `Space` | `Ctrl` + `Space` |
    | Ask the assistant | `Left Ctrl` + `Left Alt` | `Option` + `Ctrl` + `Space` | `Ctrl` + `Alt` + `Space` |

    Every shortcut is rebindable in *Settings → General → Shortcuts*. The full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/start/keyboard-shortcuts).
  </Step>
</Steps>
