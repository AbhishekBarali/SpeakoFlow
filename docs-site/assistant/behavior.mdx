Tune how the assistant carries a conversation and how long its answers run, in *Settings → Assistant → Behavior*.

## Conversation memory

*Default: 12. Range 0–200.*

How many recent messages are sent as context each turn. Higher keeps more of the thread in mind at the cost of a bigger prompt; **0** disables it and treats every message as standalone.

Cloud providers get exactly the number you ask for. Azure and local engines (the built-in one, or an Ollama/LM Studio server on loopback) also get a character cap on top, because Azure's gateway rejects oversized request bodies and a local model runs a small context window — sending your full history there would fail rather than work slowly. Turns carrying a screenshot trim harder on those same providers, since the image already dominates the budget.

## Summarize long chats

*Default: on.*

Once the un-summarized part of a conversation grows past your Conversation memory window, the oldest of those messages are folded into a rolling summary instead of being dropped, keeping the six most recent turns verbatim. The summary is injected as a context note ahead of the history, so a long chat keeps its beginning in condensed form rather than forgetting it.

The fold runs in the background after a reply finishes, so it never delays an answer, and a subtle indicator shows while it's working. If it fails, the summary is left alone and the next turn tries again.

Turn it off and a long conversation simply loses its oldest messages once they fall outside the window.

<Tip>
  You can also compact a conversation on demand by typing `/summarize` in the panel. That one replaces the visible transcript with its summary, rather than working quietly in the background.
</Tip>

## Response length

*No effect, Short, Medium, Long. Default: No effect.*

Appends a length instruction to the system prompt at request time. "No effect" adds nothing and leaves your prompt exactly as written.

Each option tells the model to still match your intent, so a greeting gets a short friendly reply even on **Long** rather than a padded one. Individual [profiles](/personalize/profiles) can override this, and the profile's choice wins for its own turns.

<Note>
  This is short-term, per-conversation context. For durable facts the assistant remembers *across* chats, see [Personal memory](/personalize/memory) — a separate, opt-in feature.
</Note>
