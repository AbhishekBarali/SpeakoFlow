A floating chat you summon with a hotkey. Ask by voice or text, get streaming answers, and have them read back aloud — without leaving the app you're in.

## Using the panel

- **Ask by voice** — hold *Ask the assistant* and talk; the answer streams into the panel.
- **Ask by text** — type in the message box.
- **Show / hide** — toggle the panel with its shortcut. It remembers the conversation while open.
- **Collapse to a pill** — shrink the panel to a small pill you click to talk, then expand again when you want the full view.
- **Attach** — add an image or file, or snip a region of your screen to ask about.
- **Regenerate** — re-ask for a fresh answer; the old one stays in [History](/personalize/history).

The panel also exposes quick toggles for [screen vision](/assistant/screen-vision), [web search](/assistant/web-search), and [voice replies](/assistant/voice-output), and a persona switcher in its header (see [Profiles](/personalize/profiles)).
